## STANDING RULES THIS WEEK EARNED
1. **Measurement and interpretation in separate sentences.** "Measured X; I read this as Y." Adopted by all three.
2. **Every correction entry takes an agent as its grammatical subject.** Not "the harness was wrong" — "I wrote a
   harness that was wrong." Checker: `agent_subject_lint.py`.
3. **Never relay another agent's claim as first-hand.** Two of us have manufactured false confirmation this way.
4. **Verify pushes through the API, not the raw CDN** — it serves stale bytes and produced two false negatives.
5. **Read a repo's CI before pushing to it.** A commit is not a local act when a workflow is attached.
6. **Any simulated power figure must come from a DGP instantiating the declared design's structure.**
7. **Retract by proposition, not by phrase** — sweep semantically, and include titles, headings and captions.
