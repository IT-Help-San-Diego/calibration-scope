## The paper
Kean H., Fung A., Jaggers P., Chen J., Rule J.S., Benn Y., Tenenbaum J.B., Piantadosi S.T.,
Varley R.A., Fedorenko E. (2026). **"Evidence from formal logical reasoning reveals that the
language of thought is not natural language."** PNAS 123(28):e2520095123.
DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2520095123. CC BY-NC-ND. (MIT BCS / McGovern; UCL; UC Berkeley; Manchester Met.)

**Method:** (1) fMRI in healthy adults during inductive + deductive logic tasks; (2) behavioral
logic testing in patients with severe aphasia (extensive language-network lesions).
**Findings (their words, verified in the text):**
- The language brain network is NOT engaged during logical reasoning.
- Patients with severe aphasia show INTACT performance on logic tasks.
- Inductive reasoning recruits the domain-general multiple-demand network; deductive reasoning
  draws on regions DISTINCT from both language and multiple-demand networks.
- Conclusion: "linguistic representations are neither utilized nor required for inductive or
  deductive logical reasoning" — logic runs on a distinct "language of thought" (LOT), not words.

## The thread (why this is a keystone, not just a nice cite)
Put the human result and calibration-scope's LLM result side by side:

| | Logic ↔ language relationship | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Humans** (Kean/Fedorenko) | SEPARABLE — distinct neural systems; lose language (aphasia), keep logic | fMRI + lesion |
| **LLMs** (calibration-scope §10.8/§10.15) | FUSED / CONFOUNDED — the SAME model's logical verdict MOVES when the linguistic carrier changes (Lean vs prose vs flattery) | Carrier Color paired measurement |

**The claim this licenses:** carrier-sensitivity is the empirical signature of the separation
LLMs LACK. Humans have logic as an architecturally distinct faculty, so a change of words
(carrier) does not move the inference — an aphasic reasons fine with the language system offline.
An LLM has logic entangled with the token stream, so a change of carrier drags the verdict. Our
"logic too late — bolted onto a language model" theory (§10.15) now has a human counterpart: the
thing bolted on late in training is precisely the thing humans have as a separate system from the
start. The tech-founder joke ("learn to talk, THEN bolt on STEM") is the same structural claim.

## Honest boundary (do NOT overclaim — Rule: argue our analogy as ours)
- The paper is about HUMAN brains. It makes NO claim about LLMs. The LLM link is calibration-scope's
  ANALOGY / HYPOTHESIS, not the authors' finding. Frame it as "we propose the carrier-sensitivity we
  measure is consistent with LLMs lacking the logic/language separation Kean et al. demonstrate in
  humans" — a hypothesis we defend, never a result we borrow.
- "Language of thought" is a specific cognitive-science construct (Fodor lineage); use it precisely,
  not as a loose metaphor.
- This does NOT prove LLMs would benefit from an architecturally separate logic module — that is a
  further, untested design conjecture. Flag it as such if raised.
